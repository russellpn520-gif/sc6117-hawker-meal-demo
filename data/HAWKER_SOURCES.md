# Nearby hawker dinner data

`hawker_orders.json` contains 25 suggested orders at 11 Singapore food courts, hawker centres and campus dining locations. A venue has either its campus/building reference coordinates or an exact NEA record name. Stalls in the same building share its coordinates. Distances are straight-line estimates from the selected landmark or the visitor’s current location. The directory covers locations within 5 km. Google Maps supplies walking directions when a visitor chooses to leave the app.

## Centre closures

`hawker_nea.csv` contains the names, coordinates, quarterly cleaning intervals and other-works intervals of the five NEA centres featured here. These fields are an extract of the National Environment Agency’s *Dates of Hawker Centres Closure*, downloaded 28 November 2026, under the Singapore Open Data Licence. Endpoints of each interval count as closure days. For example, the schedule lists Zion Riverside on 2–3 December 2026 and Maxwell on 14 December 2026. The snapshot covers the published 2026 cleaning schedule. Campus and privately run dining venues do not have entries in this extract. Admissions, stall hours, stock and prices must be checked at the venue. [NEA dataset](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_bda4baa634dd1cc7a6c7cad5f19e2d68/view) · [NEA announcements](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/announcements) · [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence)

To refresh the snapshot, retrieve the published records, select the exact `nea_name` values in the venue file, and export the existing CSV columns. Run `tests/test_nearby_hawker.py` and update the snapshot date and new schedule’s coverage label. The runtime uses these local files. [Public source records](https://data.gov.sg/api/action/datastore_search?resource_id=d_bda4baa634dd1cc7a6c7cad5f19e2d68&limit=300)

## Menus, notes and nutrition

Each card links to its venue, operator, university guide or published inspector’s notes via its `source` key. The bilingual order and tasting notes are editorial suggestions. In particular a customized order depends on what the stall can serve that day. No customer quotes, star ratings or shop-price estimates are supplied. Where a source publishes regular evening closures, `closed_evenings` lists weekdays (Monday is 0) to exclude. Opening times, public-holiday hours and temporary changes should be checked with the stall. [NTU directory](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/life-at-ntu/leisure-and-dining/general-directory) · [NUS UTown directory](https://uci.nus.edu.sg/campus-life/campus-services/retail-dining/university-town/) · [Lau Pa Sat stall directory](https://www.laupasat.sg/hawkers/) · [JW50 operator](https://www.changcheng.sg/jw50-hawker-heritage) · [NUS weekend dinner outlet and hours](https://www.waacow.sg/pages/stores-and-operating-hours) · [MICHELIN’s Singapore hawker guide](https://guide.michelin.com/en/best-of/the-best-hawker-centers-in-singapore-and-what-to-order)

`portion_items` contains editable reference grams of existing food IDs. A standard dish uses a local whole-dish reference where available. Customized plates and bowls use individual prepared-food references, including the estimated broth or cooking oil where specified. These figures estimate the proposed plate; they are not measurements of a particular stall’s recipe. Both the card and the ready draft use the same grams. [Food composition sources](SOURCES.md)

Saved allergies are compared only with the allergens covered by published declarations for that exact order. Unreported ingredients, sauces, stocks or incomplete allergen coverage disqualify the order when relevant restrictions are set. The standard campus Veggie Delite uses the Singapore manufacturer’s published six-inch multigrain recipe with the specified vegetables, no added sauce and no cheese. The filter also excludes allergens marked as possible traces. Contact with other foods during preparation remains possible; the diner needs to confirm with the counter. [Singapore product declaration](https://subwayisfresh.com.sg/menu_item/veggie-delite/)
